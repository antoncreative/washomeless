## About / Description

Washomeless turns 100 neighbours into a circle of support for one person experiencing homelessness.

Each supporter gives $30 a month. Together, that's $3,000 a month — enough to fund a 12-month program in Toronto: a safe private room, food, counselling, job training and caseworker support.

How it works:
1. Choose someone. Read real stories from people in Toronto who are ready to rebuild their lives.
2. Give $30/month. Join up to 99 others in their circle.
3. Watch them rise. The person you support shares short updates — a voice note, a photo or a few words — about twice a week, and you follow every milestone: getting keys, starting training, landing a job.

Every applicant is reviewed before their profile goes live. Updates are shared only with each person's circle of supporters, to protect their privacy and safety.

We're building Washomeless as a website and an iPhone app, starting in Toronto.

Learn more and meet the people: washomeless.com
